## 1. Description & Objectives

devops-cli measures structure at one revision only: per-function complexity in `security/complexity.py:141`, top-level symbols in `ai/ast_cache.py:20`, a regex Low/Medium/High bucket in `ai/analyze/outlines.py:163`. `devops ai analyze branch` knows each file's added/modified/deleted status (`commands/analyze.py:372-397`) but analyzes head content only, and the review pre-analysis leaves `change_type` at `existing` (`ai/review/pipeline.py:755`). The delivered construct validator invalidates a finding whose cited construct is absent from the head file (`ai/review/construct_validator.py:395`) and records it as a false positive (line 343), so a finding about a symbol the change removed is discarded: a probe finding "removal of `legacy_helper` breaks callers" came back INVALIDATED. How often real reviews hit this is unmeasured. vibes `repomap_cache.py:326-400` scores a refactor by cosine distance over eight AST features, with bands at 0.05 and 0.15 that `README.md:42-44` states without calibration data.
