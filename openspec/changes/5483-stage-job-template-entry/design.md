# Design

## Decisions

- **Two pull requests.** This change is part 1 of #4559: the manifests, the policy and Role rows and the Job entry point. Argo CD applies the manifests minutes after the merge, and the image rolls only after release-please, so the template, the policy and the Role must exist before the image of part 2 creates a stage Job. Part 2 switches the Service and removes the in-pod path.
- **The name prefix selects the rules.** The admission policy has one variable, `stage`, true when the name of the Job starts with `devops-cli-stage-`. Each rule that differs between the two kinds is one ternary, and each branch pins its kind. A Job with a stage name and the runner image is refused, and so is the reverse. The component label `roadmap-stage` is pinned to the stage branch, so the SearXNG policies can select the stage pod by it.
- **Deletes in their own policy.** `oldObject` holds the Job of a `DELETE`, and `object` is null, so the rules of a create do not apply. A second policy admits a `DELETE` by the Service only for a Job with the stage name and the stage component.
- **Retries by the Job controller only after an eviction.** Exit 75 is the `evicted` class of `attempt_kill.py`. `backoffLimit: 2` with `FailJob` on each other exit code lets the Job controller run the pod again after an eviction, a drain or a delete of the pod, and ends the Job on a result, a failure and a deadline. The Service decides the later attempts (part 2).
- **Check-out and check-in.** The Service writes the check-out (part 2). The Job writes its check-in only when `stage.job` names its own Job, so a Job that another Job replaced, or a Job that a person starts, writes nothing. The four keys are outside `STAGE_PLAN_KEYS`, so the orphan removal of the pod that runs again does not clear the check-out of its own Job.
- **The own orphan at once.** The check-out gives the item to one Job, so no other run can be in the middle of its publish. The orphan rule judges the mark of that Job at any age, and the selection of the same run does not hold the item by the branch that the writer removes first. Every other mark keeps the 3600 s rule, so a round of the Service behaves as before.
- **Budgets.** The deadline counts across the pods of a Job, so the time budget is the setting cut to the deadline of the Job, which the Service passes as a plain value, less 1800 s. The settings refuse a budget above 9000 or not above `roadmap.mcp.timeout_seconds`. The request budget is a `RequestBudget` of the pod with no record, through `set_request_gate`, as the Service holds its own.
- **A person's Job.** `devops k8s run-job --template stage` needs `--repo` and `--item`, names the Job with `RepoRef.adhoc_name`, gives it the stage labels, its name, the end of its deadline and the token repository, and refuses a second Job of the item while one runs.

## Rejected alternatives

- **A reserve in the request budget of the Service.** The issue body proposed it. It needs state that the Service and the pod share, which arrives with Valkey (#4563). A budget of the pod counts every request of the Job now.
- **`envFrom` of the whole Secret.** The runner Job takes the whole Secret. The stage Job needs 3 keys, so `secretKeyRef` of the 3 keys keeps every other key out of the pod, and the policy pins the list.
- **The `DisruptionTarget` condition in the pod.** The pod mounts no token and cannot read its own conditions (`attempt_kill.py`), so the time budget gives the class of a SIGTERM, and the Job controller reads the condition for the `Ignore` rule.
- **Wait for #5411, #4957, #5020 and #5026.** Tracker #5482 orders them before the stage step. This change runs the current `plan_stage` and `StageWriter`, whose code those items change later, so none of them blocks it (the owner asked for #4559 at once on 2026-11-13).
