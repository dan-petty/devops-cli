# Design

## Decisions

1. **The answer of the planner is the changed text of the plan.** The planner reply has no key for each finding. The planner prompt tells it to fix each finding at its place or to show in `feasibility` that the finding is no defect. Both answers change the text of the plan. `changed_texts(previous, plan)` folds the white space of each line of each text of the two plans, compares the two line lists with `difflib.SequenceMatcher` (no junk heuristic), and gives each block of new lines that a `replace` or an `insert` makes. A moved task is an insert, so a `task_order` repair is changed text. A pure delete gives no text.
2. **A later review has its own reply schema and a prompt that follows the prompt of the pass.** `LaterVerification` holds `earlier` (a list of `{number, verdict}`, with the verdict `holds` or `gone`) and `findings`. The key `earlier` is required, so a reply with no verdicts goes back once. The text `roadmap_stage_verify_later.md` follows `roadmap_stage_verify.md` only when the request holds `earlier`. The first review keeps its schema `Verification`, its prompt and its request. So the golden eval of the first review does not change.
3. **No verdict is `holds`.** An earlier finding that the reply gives no verdict stays open, as does a finding that it marks `holds` and a finding that it marks `gone` but reports again as a new finding. A verdict for a number that is not in `earlier` does nothing. A wrong reply can only keep a finding, never close one.
4. **A held finding stays open as it is.** The review that opened it checked its quote. The verdict `holds` says the defect is still in the plan, also when the revision changed the quoted text.
5. **A new finding is open only in changed text.** A finding of a later review that is open by `open_findings` is open only when its quote, with white space folded, is in a block of `answer`. Any other new finding is advice. A new finding that is the same as an earlier finding adds nothing.
6. **The rules decide on the verdicts, not on two counts.** `Judgement` holds the findings of a reply, the open findings, `fixed` (earlier findings marked `gone`) and `added` (open findings that are no earlier finding). `repair_round_due` gives a round past the setting while `len(fixed) > len(added)` of the last review. `PlanRevision.fixed` and `PlanRevision.added` compare the open findings of the review of the head with the open findings of the last review of the revision. A held finding is the same `Finding` value in each round, so the comparison follows each finding of the head through every round. `_decide` keeps a revision when `len(fixed) > len(added)`: a revision that fixes 2 findings of the head and adds 1 in changed text is better than the head.
7. **The first review of a `fix-plan` revision gets the open findings of the review of the head.** `revise` gives `_verified` the open findings of the head (`before`) as `earlier` and the plan that passed the checks as `previous`. The findings of the last plan comment go to the planner as before and are no earlier finding, because an earlier review at an earlier commit wrote them.
8. **The screen of `stage.verify_screen` acts on a later review as on a first review.** A band `continue` gives no finding, so each earlier finding is fixed.
9. **#5215 reuses this change.** Its point `fix_plan.finding_check` can take each earlier finding with its verdict, and `earlier` and `answer` as evidence. If #5215 ships second, its point judges the findings that the review marks `holds`, as the issue says. The same point, with `answer` as evidence, also covers the rebuttal check of step 3 of #5409.

## Rejected alternatives

- **A key `answers` in the planner reply, one entry for each finding.** The planner schema is also the schema of the plan record and of the plan reader (`FIELDS_NOT_IN_FILES`). A new key changes every plan run for a text that the changed text of the plan already holds.
- **One reply schema with an optional `earlier` key.** The first review would see a new key in its output schema, and the golden eval could not show that its recall stays the same.
- **A new finding is open when its quote is in the new plan and not in the old plan.** A moved task is in both plans, so a defect of a `task_order` repair would be advice. `difflib` sees the move.
- **The decision point of the rebuttal (step 3 of #5409) in this change.** It asks the question of the point of #5215. Two points with one question split the labelled data. A point in mode `shadow` changes no decision.
- **Keep the comparison of two counts.** It is the cause: two independent samples of one plan give different counts.
