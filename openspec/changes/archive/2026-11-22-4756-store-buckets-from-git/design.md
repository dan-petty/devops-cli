# Design

## Decisions

- **Scope of the item.** This change makes the platform buckets of the devops-cli workloads and their writer keys. The tenant backup buckets (`backups-*`) and their keys come from the tenant chart of `dan-petty/infra#14`, and the read-only identity `cloud-backup` comes with the cloud copy of #4770, which gives it its key. The 90-day rule for the version-review HTML waits for #4733 to name its bucket and prefix: a rule on a guessed prefix expires nothing, or other objects.
- **Scope and namespace of each CR.** Every CRD of the operator is Namespaced. The operator resolves `identityRef`, `policyRef`, the subjects of a binding and `bucketRef` in the namespace of their own CR, and it reads a key pair from a Secret in another namespace only through a grant in that namespace. So the CRs of the store live beside the `Seaweed` CR, and only the grant and the Secret live with the writer:

  | Kind | Count | Namespace | Why |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | `Bucket` | 4 | `object-store` | A `seaweedRef` in the namespace of the `Seaweed` CR needs no grant |
  | `BucketLifecyclePolicy` | 4 | `object-store` | Its `bucketRef` names a `Bucket` of its own namespace |
  | `S3Identity`, `S3Policy`, `S3PolicyBinding` | 2 each | `object-store` | The binding and the credential name them in their own namespace |
  | `S3Credentials` | 2 | `object-store` | Its `secretRef` names the Secret of the writer in the writer namespace |
  | `ResourceReferenceGrant` | 2 | `devops`, `argo-workflows` | The operator checks a cross-namespace `secretRef` against the grants of the Secret namespace |
  | Secret `object-store-<writer>` | 2 | `devops`, `argo-workflows` | A pushed row; the operator never creates a Secret in a foreign namespace |

  The tenant chart can use the same form: the store CRs in `object-store`, and the grant and the Secret in the tenant namespace.
- **Generic writer form.** Each writer `<writer>` has an `S3Identity`, an `S3Policy`, an `S3PolicyBinding` and an `S3Credentials`, all named `<writer>`, and a grant `object-store-credentials` in its namespace that admits only `S3Credentials` of namespace `object-store` to only Secret `object-store-<writer>`. Writer `devops` (namespace `devops`) works on `reports`, `reports-private` and `cache`; writer `argo` (namespace `argo-workflows`) works on `argo-artifacts`.
- **Policies.** Each `S3Policy` allows `s3:ListBucket`, `s3:ListBucketMultipartUploads` and `s3:GetBucketLocation` on its buckets, and `s3:GetObject`, `s3:PutObject`, `s3:DeleteObject`, `s3:AbortMultipartUpload` and `s3:ListMultipartUploadParts` on their objects. No statement names `*` or a bucket of another writer, and no bucket is in the policy of two identities. SeaweedFS 4.54 authorizes an identity with an attached policy by its policies alone, so a key gets `AccessDenied` on each other bucket.
- **Buckets.** Each `Bucket` sets `seaweedRef` (`seaweedfs` in `object-store`), `quota.size` with `enforce: true`, and `reclaimPolicy: Retain`. `local-path` does not enforce the size of a claim, so the quotas are the limits. `seaweedRef` is the name that the other CRDs use; `clusterRef` is its deprecated alias.
- **Anonymous read.** `anonymousRead: true` only on `reports` and `reports-private`. The operator grants the reserved identity `anonymous` the action `Read` on the bucket, which allows GET and not List. No `access` entry names `anonymous`.
- **Lifecycle.** One `BucketLifecyclePolicy` for each bucket, named as the bucket. Each has the rule `abort-incomplete-multipart` (`abortIncompleteMultipartUpload.daysAfterInitiation: 1`, no prefix). `reports-private` adds `studies` (prefix `studies/`, 90 days); `cache` and `argo-artifacts` add `expire` (7 days). No bucket named `backups-*` may have an expiration rule. The CRD has no expiration date: `expiration.days` has a minimum of 1.
- **Keys.** The keyring stays the source. Rows `devops/object-store-devops` (stack `devops`) and `argo-workflows/object-store-argo` (stack `infra`) hold `accessKey` and `secretKey`, the default fields of `S3Credentials`. Their keyring-only keys are `object_store_<writer>_access_key` (20 hex characters) and `object_store_<writer>_secret_key` (`token_urlsafe(30)`, 40 characters), the lengths of the pair that the operator generates. No workload reads them yet, so a change restarts nothing; the operator sees the change of the Secret and applies the new pair.
- **Watch scope.** `watchNamespaces: [object-store, devops, argo-workflows]`: the store and the namespace of each `secretRef`. The chart then renders its manager Role and binding in each of the three namespaces, in place of a ClusterRole over every namespace.
- **Kustomization.** `k8s/object-store/kustomization.yaml` has no `namespace` transformer, because the transformer would move the grants into `object-store`. Each object names its namespace.
- **Argo CD.** Application `object-store` applies the new files at sync-wave `0` after the operator, with `SkipDryRunOnMissingResource=true` and the retries of #4750. Its automated sync with `selfHeal` restores a changed CR, and the operator restores a changed quota or rule at its next reconcile. The project `homelab` lists `devops` and `argo-workflows` as destinations, and it ignores the two pushed Secrets as orphans.
- **Network.** No NetworkPolicy changes. The operator reaches the API server through `object-store-operator`, and the filer gRPC port 18888 and the master gRPC port 19333 through the in-namespace egress of `object-store-perimeter`. This change adds no S3 client, so no writer namespace gets the label `backup-client: "true"` yet; the item that adds a client adds the label and its egress.
- **Checks.** `tests/gate/k8s_checks/object_store_buckets.py` reads the shared render of `k8s/object-store`. Each rule is a function that returns its problems; one check applies it to the committed render, and one applies it to a fixture that breaks the rule and asserts that the problem names the object, as `object_store.py` does.

## Rejected alternatives

- **The CRs in each writer namespace, with a Secret that the operator makes.** The `S3Credentials` then gets a key pair that the operator generates, which the keyring does not hold, and the issue keeps the keyring as the source. Each `Bucket` and identity would also need a grant in `object-store` for its `seaweedRef`, and the operator would still have to watch the writer namespace (`cmd/main.go`, `Cache.DefaultNamespaces`).
- **An ESO `Password` generator for the platform keys.** No External Secrets Operator CRD is on the cluster (`kubectl get crd`, 2026-11-22). The owner chose it for the tenant keys of the tenant chart, which comes with `dan-petty/infra#12` and `#14`.
- **An empty `watchNamespaces`.** The chart then renders the manager rules as a ClusterRole over every namespace (`deploy/helm/templates/rbac/role.yaml`, else branch), with write access to the Secrets and the workloads of each namespace.
- **The present watch scope of `object-store` only.** The grant check lists the grants of the Secret namespace, and the controller gets the Secret, through the namespace cache of the manager; a namespace outside the cache gives an error, so each credential would fail.
- **`Bucket.spec.access` in place of `S3Policy`.** It grants the coarse actions `Read`, `Write`, `List`, `Tagging` and `Admin` of the legacy action list. The issue and the tenant chart use `S3Policy` and `S3PolicyBinding`, which name each S3 action, and SeaweedFS 4.54 treats attached policies as authoritative.
- **A `namespace` transformer that sets only unset namespaces.** Explicit namespaces are the plain form, and every other object of the folder already names `object-store`.
- **Buckets `backups-*` and their identities in this change.** The owner sends the tenant buckets and keys to the tenant chart of `dan-petty/infra#14`, with ESO keys and no push-secrets row.
- **The reserved identity `cloud-backup` with no key.** It would read `backups-*` buckets that do not exist yet, and #4770, which copies the store, gives it its key and uses it.
