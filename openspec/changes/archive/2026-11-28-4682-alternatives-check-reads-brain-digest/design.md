# Design

## Decisions

- **The check reads the index of `valkey-roadmap` through `BrainIndex`.** The body of the issue names `src/devops_cli/valkey/tiered.py`, the tier of `valkey.url` with the namespace of the caches, which holds no brain index. The index is in `valkey-roadmap`, and `roadmap_state_tier()` (`src/devops_cli/valkey/cache.py:398`) gives its one client in the process with its breaker. The change adds no store and no client.
- **A new module `brain_digest` builds the digest.** `brain_index` holds the keys and the commands of the store, and gets two reads, `items` and `tools`. `brain_digest` holds the digest, its cut, its entry ids and the choice of the index from the setting. `alternatives` calls `read_digest` in `_verdict_check`, before the one `investigate` call, and gives the digest to `alternatives_request`.
- **A read gives a whole hash with `HSCAN`.** The index already reads its watermark with `HSCAN` through the breaker of the tier, and `brain` may send it. The loop moves from `watermark` into `_fields`, which the three reads share. A hash of an item holds 10 fields, so one step of 500 reads it. A key that the store does not hold gives no field, so a miss and a failure stay apart.
- **An entry of a wrong form is no entry.** A hash of another schema, or with a verdict or a time that does not parse, gives no `HeldItem`. A tool hash of another schema or of another canonical id gives no `HeldTool`. A near item with no entry is left out of the digest. A person can change a record, and the index copies it, so no value makes a read raise.
- **The digest of one item: at most 11 reads.** The entry of the item, then its near items in one call of `items`, then the distinct tools of the near items in one call of `tools`. Each command goes through the breaker, so after the first failure the next commands are not sent.
- **Any failed read gives the empty digest.** One read that the store does not answer gives `None`, and the digest is empty with one log line. A digest from a part of the reads would mix entries of two times of the store. The check runs as before (ADR 0023 section 1).
- **The date of a near item is the date of its check.** The item hash holds one time, `checked_at`, for the verdict and the tool of the check. The digest gives it as a date in `checked`, as the block of a check gives its date.
- **The ADR status comes from the ADRs that the check reads.** `rejected_by` names each ADR of the list `adrs` of the run, the ADRs in force that fit the ADR cap, for which `rejects` is true with the canonical id and the name of the tool. These are the ADRs of `decisions`, so the model can read each one that the digest names.
- **The cut measures the JSON text of the digest.** The request writes the digest as a JSON list of objects. The cut counts the tokens of that list with `count_tokens`, in the order of the near items, and leaves out a whole entry that does not fit. A later entry that fits stays, as in `fit_to_budget` of the ADRs.
- **The ids of the used entries.** `item:<n>` for the entry of an item and `tool:<h16>` for the entry of a tool, the kind and the last part of the key, as the tests of #4676 wrote them. The list starts with the entry of the item, whose `near` field gave the near items, then each near item and its tool. It is empty for an empty digest and holds at most `CONST_ROADMAP_BRAIN_ITEM_MAX_USES` ids.
- **`alternatives.brain_used` is a mark of the one write.** `AlternativesWriter.settle` adds it to the marks of `set_marks`, beside the block. A check with no used entry clears an earlier value and writes no key when the record holds none, as `alternatives.refs` does. Declared in `AlternativesRecordKey`, the key is kept by `decode_job_record`, so the sync copies it to `uses`.
- **`roadmap.brain.digest` is a new section `roadmap.brain`.** The type is `Literal["off", "on"]`, as the issue gives it, with the variable `DEVOPS_CLI_ROADMAP_BRAIN_DIGEST`. `plan_alternatives` reads it once for a run with the other settings. `off` gives no index, so no command goes to the store.
- **No ring and no area verdict.** The index holds neither, and the change writes no new kind of key: an area check is a task of #4693, and the ring is #7389. The spec says that the digest holds neither while the index holds neither.
- **The eleventh requirement of the issue is the place of the spec.** The delta is in `openspec/specs/project-brain/digest/spec.md`, and `roadmap-alternatives` keeps its 80 requirements. The documents group verifies it with `uv run devops openspec check` and a count of the requirements.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Read through the tiered mapping of `src/devops_cli/valkey/tiered.py`.** Rejected: that tier is the cache of `valkey.url` (`valkey-devops`), and `BrainIndex` keys are in `valkey-roadmap` (`src/devops_cli/roadmap/brain_index.py`, key table). A read there finds no entry.
- **A method `hgetall` or a pipeline of reads on the tier.** Rejected: `ValkeyCacheTier.hscan` (`src/devops_cli/valkey/cache.py:176`) already reads a hash through the breaker and counts its errors, and at most 11 small hashes are read for an item. A new method adds code and a contract case for no measured gain.
- **A digest from a part of the reads when one read fails.** Rejected: one failure opens the breaker for 60 seconds (`DEFAULT_VALKEY_CACHE_BREAKER_COOLDOWN_SECONDS`), so the next reads are not sent either. A part would show the near items without their tools.
- **A store or a key kind for the area verdict.** Rejected: ADR 0023 section 3 lists the area verdicts as insights of class D, which the tasks of #4693 write. This change writes no key, and a key with no writer would hold nothing.
- **The digest as one text string, as `decisions` is.** Rejected: the scenario of the issue counts the entries of `brain`, and a list of objects keeps the date, the verdict and the tool of each near item apart for the model. A digest of 5 entries holds about 360 tokens in this form (a count with `count_tokens` of a fixture of 5 entries).
- **A digest entry for the item itself.** Rejected: the item is the subject of the check, and its block already holds its last verdict, which the request leaves out on purpose (`alternatives_request` reads the body outside the block).
- **A count of the digest reads in `devops_cli_brain_reads_total`.** Rejected: the counter has no value for a read with no entry, and the values `answered` and `partial` of the first reads are #7388. The record `alternatives.brain_used` and the log line of a failed read show what the digest read.
