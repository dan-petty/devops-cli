### Fixed

- An inline suppression marker hides only findings of the tool it belongs to (#1341): `nosec` for Bandit, `nosemgrep` or `no-semgrep` for Semgrep, `noqa` for Ruff. A `# noqa` no longer hides a Bandit or Semgrep finding, and a `# nosec` no longer hides a Semgrep finding. A tool without a marker of its own is never suppressed inline.
